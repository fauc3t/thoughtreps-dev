How it works

Write it once. See it again.

  1. 1

    Write it down

    Tap + from anywhere and start typing. A thought takes about five seconds to save.

  2. 2

    Let it wait

    It stays out of your way for 7 days. Change the wait for any thought, from one day to a month.

  3. 3

    It comes back

    It shows up on your timeline. Read it and it goes back in the queue for next time. Done with it? Archive it.

  • No serverEverything is saved on your iPhone. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Export anytimeSave everything as a .zip of JSON and images, or as Markdown files.
  • iCloud sync LaterOptional sync between your devices is on the way.
