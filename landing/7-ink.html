How it works

Write it once. See it again in a week.

Most notes apps are where ideas go to be forgotten. Thought Reps only shows you what's due today, and brings the rest back on schedule.

  1. 1

    Write it

    Tap + from anywhere. Markdown, photos and #tags all work, and it takes about five seconds.

    Rubber-duck before askingsaved
  2. 2

    Let it go

    It stays off your timeline for 7 days, or whatever interval you set for that thought.

    Rubber-duck before askingin 4 days
  3. 3

    Meet it again

    It's back on your timeline, marked due. Read it and it heads out for another lap. Archive it when you're done.

Privacy

Your thoughts never leave your phone.

There's no account and no server. Back it up whenever you like, as files you own.

  • No accountOpen the app and start writing.
  • Stored on your iPhoneText and images stay in the app, on your device.
  • Export any timeA .zip of JSON and images, or one Markdown file per thought.
  • iCloud syncLaterOptional sync across your devices is planned.
