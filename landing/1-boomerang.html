How it works

Three steps, and then it loops.

Most notes apps are where ideas go to sit. Thought Reps keeps them moving: you write, it waits, it brings them back.

  1. 1Capture

    Write it in under 5 seconds

    Tap the + from any tab. The editor opens with the keyboard up. Markdown, images and #tags all work.

    ## Rubber-duck before asking
    Explain the bug out loud first. #work
  2. 2Wait

    It steps out of the way

    New thoughts stay off your timeline until they come due. No pile of unread notes staring at you.

    nextDueAt = createdAt + 7 days
  3. 3Comes back

    Read it, and it goes again

    Due thoughts show up on your Timeline, oldest first. Open one and it heads back out for another round.

    viewed · back on Oct 11

Not ready for it yet? Swipe to snooze for a day or a week. That doesn't count as a view.
