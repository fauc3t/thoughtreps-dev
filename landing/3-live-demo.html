How it works

Write it once. See it again in a week.

Most notes apps are where ideas go to be forgotten. Thought Reps keeps your timeline short by only showing what's due today.

Day 0Day 7
Day 0

Write it down

Tap + and type. Markdown, photos and #tags all work. It takes about five seconds.

Days 1 to 6

Let it rest

The thought stays out of sight. Your timeline only shows what's due, so it never piles up.

Day 7

It comes back

It's on your timeline again. Read it and it goes away for another week. Pin it to keep it around every day.

Features

Small app. Everything a thought needs.

Markdown and photos

Headings, lists, code, tables and images. Write the way you think.

#tags

Type #anything in a thought. Every tag gets its own timeline.

#swift#reading#gifts#habits

Pin the keepers

Keep a few thoughts at the top every day, like your weekly review questions.

Blurred blocks

Hide an answer under a blur and tap to reveal it. Good for quizzing yourself.

Snooze or archive

Not today? Push it back a day or a week. Done with it? Archive it and restore it any time.

Your own rhythm

Every 7 days by default. Set any thought to come back daily, monthly, or anywhere between.

Daily reminder

One nudge in the morning.

Pick a time and Thought Reps tells you how many thoughts are back. If nothing is due, it stays quiet.

Privacy

It all stays on your iPhone.

  • No accountOpen the app and start writing. There's nothing to sign up for.
  • No serverYour thoughts and photos are stored on the phone and nowhere else.
  • Export any timeSave a .zip of everything to Files or AirDrop, or export plain Markdown files.
  • iCloud sync laterComing in a future update, and it will be optional.
