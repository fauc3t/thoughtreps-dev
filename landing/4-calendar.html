What’s inside

Everything you saw this week.

Markdown and images

Headings, lists, code, tables and photos. Write it the way you think it.

#tags, each with a timeline

Type #study anywhere in a thought. Every tag gets its own timeline.

Pins

Keep a few thoughts at the top of your Timeline every day.

Blurred blocks

Hide an answer under a blur. Tap to reveal it when you’re ready to check yourself.

Snooze and archive

Swipe to push a thought back, or archive it when you’re done. You can restore it anytime.

Your own intervals

Every 7 days by default. Set any thought to 1 day, 30 days, or anything in between.

Private by default

Your thoughts stay on your iPhone.

There’s no account to make and no server holding your notes. Export everything whenever you like.

  • AccountNone. Open the app and start writing.
  • StorageOn your device, including images.
  • ExportA .zip of JSON and images, or one Markdown file per thought.
  • SynciCloud sync is coming in a later update.
